When Machines Changed the Job

Nineteen historical technology transitions, the patterns that repeat across all of them, and what that implies for organisations adopting AI agents now — with four interactive simulations built to test the policy questions it raises.

Companion instruments — Appendix A
I

Surprise Circumplex

What one person feels when a habit is interrupted. Arousal and valence are computed independently, so the same event scatters a population across every emotional quadrant rather than moving everyone the same way.

Open →
II

The Innovator's Wake

How adoption spreads across a population over time. A leading edge turns out to be sustained by succession, not distance — replacing retirees at the frontier is what keeps it a frontier.

Open →
III

The Second Tier

Whether training the laggards is a good use of resources. Being able to hand work down was worth ~30% of output; training budget was worth approximately nothing.

Open →
IV

The Capability Gate

A write-up, not a simulation: what organisational attributes actually produce a capable second tier, and what data to collect before spending anything on it.
